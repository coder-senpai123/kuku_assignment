## Quality Check: Where Does the Tool Pick Badly?
Automated screenplay analysis has intrinsic boundaries. Below is a detailed audit of where algorithmic selection fails and how human editors should inspect the cuts:

### 1. The 'Stage Direction' Illusion (Passive Action False Positive)
- **The Pitfall**: Script descriptions like 'Juliana clenches her knuckles with suppressed fury' or 'Her eyes fill with silent sorrow' read with high dramatic weight on paper. On screen, 4 seconds of silent physical reaction without rapid dialogue or camera movement translates to sluggish video pacing, triggering instant thumb scrolls.
- **Audience Impact**: High bounce rate in first 2 seconds on TikTok / Instagram Reels.
- **Algorithmic Mitigation**: Enforce presence of punchy spoken dialogue or dynamic camera motion (extreme close-up / sudden snap) within the first 1.5 seconds.

### 2. The Cold-Audience Lore Trap (Context Asymmetry)
- **The Pitfall**: Episodes 30-50 feature intense confrontations, but they hinge on intricate subplots (e.g., poisoned dowries, secondary noble factions, forged wills). The tool detects explosive keywords ('poison', 'traitor', 'proof'), but cold viewers scrolling social feeds have zero emotional investment in secondary characters.
- **Audience Impact**: High initial click-through but catastrophic drop-off before 10 seconds; low app installation conversion.
- **Algorithmic Mitigation**: Apply a steep decay penalty to episodes beyond Episode 20 for top-of-funnel ads, reserving them strictly for mid-campaign retargeting.

### 3. Over-Indexing on Scriptwriter 'Cliffhanger' SFX Tags
- **The Pitfall**: Nearly every episode ends with a variation of 'Solemn low-pitched string drone swells to an ominous cliffhanger peak. Cut to black.' The tool can be tricked into picking a dull 30-second lead-up simply because the terminal SFX cue contains the literal word 'cliffhanger'.
- **Audience Impact**: Promos that start weakly but end with a loud boom and cut to black, boring viewers long before the cliffhanger arrives.
- **Algorithmic Mitigation**: Couple cliffhanger scoring with dialogue tension and unresolved character questions, rather than relying solely on SFX cues.

### 4. Multi-Segment Visual & Audio Discontinuity
- **The Pitfall**: Stitching two segments from different scenes or episodes (e.g. Cathedral altar to rainy woods) can create jarring tonal dissonance. Without a crossfade or audio bridge, a cold viewer experiences cognitive whiplash.
- **Audience Impact**: Viewer confusion regarding character location, wardrobe, and timeline.
- **Algorithmic Mitigation**: Restrict stitched promos to segments that share identical location headings or involve immediate cause-and-effect character actions.

### 5. Micro-Boundary Clipping & Cadence Chopping
- **The Pitfall**: Automated time boundaries that cut off an actor mid-breath, mid-sentence, or right as the background score resolves rather than holding the unresolved tension.
- **Audience Impact**: The promo feels mechanically truncated or defective.
- **Algorithmic Mitigation**: Snap candidate boundaries strictly to beat ends and use LLM offset refinement to ensure natural sentence cadences.

### Recommendations for Editors
- Always preview the first 2 seconds on actual mobile vertical frame (9:16) to verify that physical action reads dynamically without relying on reading stage directions.
- For cold acquisition on TikTok, prioritize Episodes 1-4 promos where the emotional premise is universally self-explanatory (wedding altar abandonment).
- When using stitched promos across different locations, add a quick 0.5s audio bridge or whoosh sound effect so viewers perceive it as a deliberate trailer cut rather than a playback error.
- Avoid using promos from late episodes (> Ep 30) for cold audiences; deploy them exclusively as retargeting ads to viewers who have already completed Episode 5.
